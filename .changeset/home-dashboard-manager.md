Home is now a dashboard. It starts with "Needs attention", shown only when something does, most urgent first: a job that did not finish (View log), an app that is not responding (Check again), app updates (Update on each, Update all when several can start, Review when one needs your approval), anything your account still needs for the apps you have (Set up, or Not needed to hide it in this browser), the deploy-copy cleanup, and the notice that an older Appflare runs a newer database. Below it, "Your apps" shows a card for every app with its name, one line on how it is doing, Open and Manage. The installs table is gone, and Home no longer shows Worker names.

The sidebar lists your apps under "Your apps", by name, each with a dot when it needs attention, at most eight at a time with the rest scrolling, and a search icon that turns into a filter (Escape clears it). The number beside Home counts what needs attention.

With no app installed, opening Appflare goes straight to the catalog; a click on Home still shows Home, with a way to the catalog.
