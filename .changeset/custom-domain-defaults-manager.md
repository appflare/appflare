Apps open at their primary address. The home list, the app page and job pages have an "Open" button that opens the app in a new tab at the domain that answered when workers.dev was turned off, else its first live custom domain, else its first live external domain, else its workers.dev URL. The Worker name is shown as plain text. A domain counts as live once a request through it reaches the app.

A live domain now turns workers.dev off by default. When a custom or external domain first answers as the app (the install's domain step, or a check on the app's Domains and email tab, which now checks a new custom domain on its own every 10 seconds while the page is open), Appflare turns the Worker's workers.dev URL off, keeping version previews on so update checks keep working, and the app page says "workers.dev turned off because a domain is live" beside the existing switch. Removing the last live domain turns workers.dev back on first. Once an admin uses the switch on an app, Appflare leaves it as they set it, and removing that app's last live domain while workers.dev is off is refused as before. workers.dev also stays on when the Worker's settings were filled in with its workers.dev URL (`{{workerUrl}}`), since they would point nowhere; the app page explains how to move them to the domain. Apps installed before this keep their switch as it is when they already have a domain. The database upgrade marks every existing custom domain live without asking the app through it, along with external domains Cloudflare last reported active and the domain workers.dev was turned off for; a custom domain that does not serve the app can then be where "Open" goes until it is removed. When the domain the app is served on is removed, another live domain takes its place for health checks, update checks and `{{workerUrl}}`, which now prefer live domains over ones still waiting for their certificate or records. The install form says "workers.dev will be turned off once the domain is live" when an address is chosen.
