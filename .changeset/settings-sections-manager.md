Every settings page now reads the same way: a title with one line under it, then sections, each with a short heading, one sentence on what it is for, its main action at the right, and everything else in a single card below. Lists of catalogs and notification channels become rows of one card, and the danger zone's actions are rows of their own.

Messages that used to spell out a path through the menus ("Settings > Account and capabilities > GitHub access") now link straight to the section they mean, including the errors a failed job or a refused action shows. When you are in the middle of something, such as the install form or the setup screens, the link opens in a new tab. Arriving from such a link scrolls to the section and briefly outlines it.
