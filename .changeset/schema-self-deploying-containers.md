`SANDBOX_CONTAINERS` lists the sandbox Worker's two new container classes for the installers of self-deploying apps, with the container applications `appflare-sandbox-self-deploying-standard-1` and `appflare-sandbox-self-deploying-standard-2` (up to two instances each), and every entry now says which runs it serves (`use`: `build` or `self-deploying`).
