The new Appflare logo everywhere: the sidebar shows the full logo on its own, the sign-in and setup pages show it above the card, and the favicon and touch icon use the new mark (white in a dark browser theme, the cloud always orange).

The sidebar footer is now a compact account menu: your initial, and when opened your name, email and role, links to Users and access and to your passkeys, and Sign out. Next to it, Appflare's own version, muted and linking to Settings, Appflare updates. When a newer release is out, a small card above the footer shows it with an Update button that runs the update right there, shows its progress and result in the card, and reloads the page onto the new version; while Appflare is up to date there is no card. Settings no longer carries a count for Appflare's update, and the Home count covers apps only.

Home offers the pending app updates directly: with one, an Update button in the banner and on its row; with several, a button on each row and Update all, which starts every update that needs nothing from you and lists the ones that need a secret, a confirmation or an approval, each linked to its app's page. Update all follows the same rules as automatic updates, so builds to approve and versions that failed or were rolled back before are listed rather than started.
