"@appflare/manager": minor

Apps of more than three Workers now install, update, roll back, change settings and uninstall on Workers Free, up to the 24 Workers a catalog entry may have. Cloudflare lets a job make 50 requests at a time on Workers Free, and a job only gets a fresh allowance after a pause of 5 minutes (measured: shorter pauses, step retries and waiting for an event all keep the old one). A job for an app of several Workers on an account not known to be on Workers Paid now counts its requests, and before a step that might not fit what is left it pauses for 5 minutes, says so in its log, and carries on in a fresh allowance; every step still runs once, in the same order. The steps that switch every Worker to a version (an update's promotion, a settings change, a rollback, and putting Workers back after a failed update) run in one allowance, with the pause before them if needed, so the app's Workers never serve different versions for minutes; so do the probes of a preview or health check, so a pause never eats into the check's time. A step that runs out of the job's own allowance anyway runs once more after such a pause instead of failing the job; one that a job unit runs out of fails as before. Jobs of apps of one Worker, and every job on Workers Paid, run as before. The install and update estimate in the job log now counts these pauses, and the refusal of a free app of more than three Workers is gone; the account's limit of 100 Workers on Workers Free is still checked before anything is created.
