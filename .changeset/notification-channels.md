"@appflare/manager": minor

Notification channels: the manager can tell Telegram, Slack, Discord or your own webhook when an update becomes available, an update is applied or fails, an install or uninstall finishes, an installed app's health check starts failing, or a newer Appflare release is out. Admins add, edit, test and remove channels under Settings, Notification channels, and pick the events for each.

Telegram takes a bot token and a chat id; Slack and Discord take an incoming webhook URL. A generic webhook receives a JSON body with an `X-Appflare-Signature: sha256=<hex>` header, the HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body keyed with a signing secret the manager generates and shows once (it can be replaced later). Receivers should verify the signature over the raw bytes before parsing them, reject a body whose `sentAt` is more than a few minutes old, and ignore an `id` they have already seen, since a delivery can be retried. Webhook addresses must be `https://`, redirects are not followed, and the address is not checked against private ranges: a Worker cannot reach private networks, and only admins can add channels. Credentials are encrypted in D1 with AES-GCM under a key derived from the Worker's `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`, are never shown again, and never appear in messages, logs or error text. Messages name the app, its version and Worker, and link to the manager; a failed job links to its log instead of quoting its error.

A job's end is sent right away from its last step, through the manager's own `SELF` binding so the job spends one subrequest on it. Update availability, Appflare releases and failing health checks are sent by the scheduled run, each once per install and version, once per release, or once per failing episode. While a channel wants health alerts, the scheduled run probes every installed app the same way "Check now" does; an episode starts only when that check gets two server errors in a row, and ends only when a check finds the app serving again (no answer at all, such as a timeout, neither starts nor ends one). A delivery that fails in a way a retry may fix is retried with backoff for up to five attempts within a day, and Settings shows each channel's failed attempts since its last delivery and the last error.
