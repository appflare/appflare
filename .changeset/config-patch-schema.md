Catalog entries may carry `install.configPatch` (and `install.workers[].configPatch` for an app of several Workers): a JSON merge patch (RFC 7386) the packer applies to the app's wrangler config before wrangler reads it, signed with the rest of the manifest. Only `main`, `assets`, `build` (only `null`), `services`, `kv_namespaces`, `r2_buckets`, `d1_databases`, `vars` (only removals) and `migrations` may be set; any other key, `__proto__` included, is refused with a message saying why. A patched `main` or `assets.directory` must be relative to the config, not absolute and without `..`. `configPatchProblems()` checks a patch against the config it changes: service bindings may only be left out or point at a Worker of the same entry, storage lists may only add bindings or leave out an empty `id`, `bucket_name` or `database_id`, and migrations may only rename `new_classes` to `new_sqlite_classes`. `applyMergePatch()`, `patchWranglerConfig()` and `configPatchDiff()` apply a patch and describe its effect. `install.configPatch` is refused beside `install.workers` and on a self-deploying entry. A sandbox build's catalog manifest checks the patch with the same rules, and `SANDBOX_FEATURE_CONFIG_PATCH` names the sandbox Worker feature that applies it.
