A complete favicon set: an SVG favicon that turns white in a dark browser theme, PNG and ICO fallbacks for browsers without SVG favicons, a touch icon and title for the iOS home screen, and a web app manifest with 192 and 512 pixel icons, so Appflare can be installed as an app with its own name, icon and colours. The icons also show on the sign-in page and on the page shown when Cloudflare Access refuses a request.
