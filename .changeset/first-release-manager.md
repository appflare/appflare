The first public release of Appflare, a self-hosted app manager that runs as one Worker in your own Cloudflare account. It installs apps from the Appflare catalog, creates the databases, storage and other resources each app needs, and keeps the apps updated, with rollback to an earlier version. Before anything reaches your account, it checks the signature of every release it installs and the hash of every file in it. Apps can move to your own domains and be removed again together with the resources they created. Several people can share one manager, the manager itself can sit behind Cloudflare Access, and it updates itself from its own signed releases. Appflare itself can live on a domain of your account, chosen during setup or later in Settings; its workers.dev address stays and sends page requests to the new one, and passkeys keep working at the address they were added at. Pages load in few round trips, catalog images keep their place while they load, and busy buttons and the loading indicator show Appflare's own mark. The Documentation link in the account menu opens appflare.dev.
