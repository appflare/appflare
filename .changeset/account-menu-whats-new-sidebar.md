The account menu gains "What's new", Documentation, Feedback, "Give us a star" and Appearance. "What's new" shows the notes of Appflare's latest releases, newest first, marking the ones you have not read yet and the version running here; a count on the menu item and a dot on your avatar say how many are unread until you open it. The notes are read by the same half-hourly check that looks for Appflare updates and kept in the manager, so your browser never contacts GitHub. Feedback opens a new issue on the repository with a short template and the version filled in, and nothing else about your manager.

Appflare now starts in light mode. Appearance in the account menu switches to dark, or to System to follow the browser, and the choice is remembered in this browser and applied before the page first paints.

The sidebar folds into an icon rail with the button next to the logo, and remembers that in this browser; while folded, each item shows its name on hover, Home keeps a dot for pending app updates, Appflare's own update is one icon, and the account menu carries the version. Below 768 pixels wide the sidebar becomes a drawer, opened from a top bar with the logo and closed by choosing a page, Escape or a tap outside it.
