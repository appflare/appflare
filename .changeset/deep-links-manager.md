Messages that point somewhere in Appflare now link there instead of describing the way. Job logs, errors and notices link to the exact settings section, or to the right part of an app's page: its settings, secrets, databases, domains, workers.dev address, automatic updates, versions, health or danger zone. An app's page opens the tab that holds the linked part, scrolls to it and highlights it. The locked-out page links to the Cloudflare Access settings, the danger zone's refusal pages show their links, and notifications about an app's health or external domains open that part of its page.
