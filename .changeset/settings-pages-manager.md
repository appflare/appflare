Settings has a new set of pages: Your account, Building apps, Updates, Users and sign-in, Domains, Notifications, Catalogs, Removed apps and Usage data. The General page is gone. Building apps from source and GitHub access have their own page, automatic app updates sit with Appflare's own version on Updates, and the danger zone is at the bottom of Your account, shown only to the owner. Removed apps appears in the list only while an uninstalled app still keeps something.

Settings in the sidebar now opens its list of pages in place instead of leaving the page you are on, opens by itself on a settings page, and remembers whether you left it open. With the sidebar folded, the gear opens a menu of the same pages. On a phone, each settings page has a list of the other pages under its title.

Old addresses and links in older messages still work: `/settings` opens Your account, and links to the pages and sections that moved open them in their new place.
