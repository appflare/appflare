Disabling sandbox builds now leaves them off. The job deletes the sandbox Worker before it disconnects Appflare, and once that Worker is gone Cloudflare keeps Appflare's `SANDBOX` binding with no service name. The job's last step did not count that binding as Appflare's own, so it stayed: Settings > Building apps showed "Not answering" with no way back to off, and enabling again stopped at "already has a service binding named SANDBOX". Now disabling removes such a binding and enabling replaces it. While one is there, Building apps reads sandbox builds as off for everyone and offers admins Disable to finish, and installing or building an app that needs the sandbox Worker turns sandbox builds on first, as it does when they are off. Disabling is also refused before anything is deleted when its last step could not disconnect Appflare (a gradual deployment of Appflare's Worker, or a newer version uploaded but not deployed). The Disable confirmation now gives the steps in the order they run. If an earlier version already left this binding, updating Appflare stops with `The running Worker's service binding SANDBOX points at "(no service)", not at the sandbox Worker ("appflare-sandbox"); Appflare needs SANDBOX for sandbox builds. Remove or rename that binding first.` Remove the `SANDBOX` binding once in the Cloudflare dashboard (Workers & Pages, your Appflare Worker, Settings > Bindings), then update.
