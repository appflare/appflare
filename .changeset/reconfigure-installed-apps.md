Change an installed app's settings and secrets without reinstalling it. An app's page has a new Settings section: its settings, with the labels and help from the catalog, a "Set new value" for each secret (current values are never shown; a secret with generated values gets a fresh one to copy), "Remove" for a secret the installed version no longer uses, and, for an app that receives email, moving it to another zone. "Save and redeploy" starts a settings change job and opens its log; it is refused while another job of the app runs.

The job redeploys the version the app runs, never a newer one: it takes a snapshot (now including the settings), uploads the installed release again with the new settings and every binding sent explicitly while existing secrets carry over, then sets and removes secrets on that version with the versions secrets API (the call `wrangler versions secret put` makes), checks the result on its preview URL where Cloudflare allows it, and only then switches traffic to it. A repeated step reuses the version it already made instead of patching twice, and secret values stay out of the job record and its log. A later upload copies the secrets of the Worker's newest version, not of the one serving, so a change that fails before switching traffic gives the newest version the serving secrets back and says so in its log. An app built in the account's sandbox Worker redeploys the build stored there, without building again. An app that deploys itself runs its own installer again at the installed commit, with new secret values stored on the sandbox Worker first and a log of its own; its secrets can be replaced but not removed.

Moving email deploys nothing: the new zone's routes are set up first, then the old zone's are removed. If that removal stops, the Settings section says so and offers "Finish moving email", which checks the new zone again and removes what is left. Rolling back to a snapshot now also puts back the settings recorded with it, and the install's list of secrets follows the secrets the rolled-back version has. A rollback does not move email back; its dialog says so.
