Sign in with a passkey. Settings has a new Passkeys section where you add a passkey under a name of your choice, see when each one was added, and remove the ones you no longer use. The sign-in page offers "Sign in with a passkey" next to email and password, and says so plainly when the browser does not support passkeys or no passkey was used. Passkeys belong to the manager's own address, so one added on one hostname does not work on another. Email and password sign-in and first-run setup are unchanged.
